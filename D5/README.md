## Overview

The **Client Profitability and Lifecycle Analysis Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of client-level financial performance, servicing costs, profitability quality, and lifecycle trends across Morgan Stanley’s client base.

Designed in **Power BI** using a synthetic financial dataset, this dashboard focuses on understanding:

- How profitable different clients and segments are after accounting for servicing costs  
- Which client tiers, regions, and risk profiles drive sustainable profitability  
- How client profitability evolves over time across the client lifecycle  
- Whether higher-risk clients deliver proportionally higher financial returns  

The dashboard is intended for **senior leadership, wealth management heads, strategy teams, relationship managers, and operations stakeholders** to monitor client-level economics, identify high-value and underperforming segments, and support data-driven client strategy decisions.

Through a combination of **executive KPIs, lifecycle trend analysis, segment-level profitability views, and risk-aware analytics**, the dashboard supports informed, profit-focused decision-making.

---

## Business Storyline & Analytical Flow

### 1. Executive Snapshot – Client Profitability KPIs

The dashboard begins with **five KPI cards** that provide an instant executive-level snapshot of client profitability:

- **Total Revenue (USD):** Total revenue generated across the selected client base  
- **Total Cost to Serve (USD):** Aggregate servicing costs associated with clients  
- **Net Client Profit (USD):** Revenue minus cost to serve, representing true client profitability  
- **Average Revenue per Client (USD):** Revenue efficiency at the individual client level  
- **Client Profit Margin (%):** Percentage of revenue retained as profit after servicing costs  

These KPIs establish both the **scale and quality of client earnings**, enabling leadership to quickly assess profitability, cost efficiency, and margin sustainability.

---

### 2. Risk-Aware Client Profitability – Scatter Analysis

A **scatter plot**, *Client Profitability vs Risk Profile*, maps:

- **Net Client Profit** against **Client Risk Profile**  
- Bubble size representing **Assets Under Management (AUM)**  
- Color segmentation by **Client Tier**  

This visual enables identification of high-value, high-risk clients, as well as underperforming segments requiring strategic attention.

---

### 3. Segment-Level Profitability – Client Tier Comparison

A **bar chart**, *Net Client Profit by Client Tier*, provides a clear comparison of profitability across client segments. This allows leadership to:

- Identify which tiers generate the highest net value  
- Prioritize advisor focus and servicing strategies  
- Assess cost-to-serve effectiveness by segment  

---

### 4. Lifecycle Perspective – Profitability Trends Over Time

A **line chart**, *Net Client Profit Trend Over Time*, introduces a lifecycle dimension by showing how client profitability evolves across reporting periods. This highlights:

- Improving or declining profitability trends  
- Volatility or stability in client earnings  
- Early warning signals for margin erosion  

---

### 5. Revenue Concentration – Client Tier Contribution

A **donut chart**, *Revenue Contribution by Client Tier*, visualizes how total revenue is distributed across client segments. This helps assess:

- Revenue concentration risk  
- Dependency on specific client tiers  
- Diversification of the client revenue base  

---

### 6. Slicers – Interactive Filtering

All visuals in the dashboard are fully interactive and can be filtered using slicers:

- **Reporting Month:** Lifecycle and time-based analysis  
- **Region:** Geographic segmentation  
- **Client Tier:** Client segment filtering  
- **Risk Profile:** Risk-based profitability assessment

## Key Takeaways

This dashboard delivers a **structured, executive-ready view** of client profitability and lifecycle dynamics. It enables stakeholders to:

- Understand true client profitability after servicing costs  
- Identify high-value and underperforming client segments

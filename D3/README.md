## Overview

The **Investment Banking Revenue & Deal Pipeline Dashboard** provides a consolidated view of investment banking revenue performance, growth trends, client composition, geographic contribution, and associated risk exposure within a global investment banking context.

Designed in **Power BI** using a large synthetic financial dataset, this dashboard focuses on understanding:

- How investment banking revenue is generated and evolves over time  
- How revenue is distributed across regions and client tiers  
- Whether high-revenue areas are accompanied by elevated compliance and AML risk  
- Risk-adjusted revenue performance and client concentration  

The dashboard is intended for investment banking leadership, regional heads, risk and compliance teams, and strategy stakeholders to monitor revenue momentum, concentration risk, client dependence, and risk-adjusted performance. Through a combination of **executive KPIs, trend analysis, segmentation-based visuals, and risk overlays**, it supports informed decision-making and sustainable revenue management.  

---

## Business Storyline & Analytical Flow

### 1. Executive Snapshot – Investment Banking KPIs
The dashboard opens with **four KPI cards** providing an executive-level snapshot:

- **Total Investment Banking Revenue (USD):** Overall revenue generated from investment banking activities  
- **IB Revenue Contribution:** Percentage contribution of investment banking revenue to total firm revenue  
- **Average IB Revenue per Client:** Mean revenue generated per client  
- **IB Revenue Volatility:** Standard deviation of investment banking revenue, indicating stability and risk  

These KPIs establish the **scale and resilience** of the investment banking business and allow leadership to quickly gauge revenue magnitude, growth momentum, and client concentration.

---

### 2. Temporal Dynamics – Revenue Trends
A **line chart**, **Investment Banking Revenue Trend**, shows the evolution of revenue over time:

- Highlights growth patterns and cyclical trends  
- Reveals shifts in deal activity and potential early warning signals  
- Supports dynamic filtering through time-based slicers  

---

### 3. Geographic Analysis – Regional Contribution
A **clustered bar chart**, **Investment Banking Revenue by Region**, breaks down revenue by geography:

- Identifies strong and weak regions  
- Assesses geographic concentration risk  
- Supports regional benchmarking and strategic allocation  

---

### 4. Client Segmentation – Revenue Distribution
Two visuals focus on client composition:

- **IB Revenue Mix by Client Tier (Donut Chart):** Shows proportional contribution of each client tier to total revenue, highlighting concentration and dependency  
- **IB Revenue by Client Tier (Stacked Column Chart):** Breaks down revenue by client tier segmented by region, combining client and geographic perspectives  

---

### 5. Risk Analysis – Revenue vs AML Exposure
A **scatter plot**, **Investment Banking Revenue vs AML Risk**, plots revenue against average AML risk scores:

- Bubble size represents total AUM per client  
- Provides a **risk-adjusted view of revenue generation**  
- Helps identify high-revenue areas with elevated compliance or regulatory risk  

---

### 6. Slicers – Interactive Filtering
All visuals are dynamic and can be filtered using:

- **Year & Quarter:** Filter by reporting period  
- **Client Tier:** Filter by client segment  
- **Region:** Filter by geography

## Key Takeaways

This dashboard delivers a **structured, executive-ready view** of investment banking performance. It enables stakeholders to:

- Understand how investment banking revenue is generated and distributed  
- Assess growth, client concentration, and regional performance  
- Identify high-risk areas that may require oversight  
- Make informed, risk-adjusted strategic decisions  

By combining **high-level KPIs, revenue trends, regional and client segmentation, proportional revenue composition, and risk-aware analytics**, the dashboard provides a clear, actionable understanding of investment banking revenue dynamics.

With **interactive slicers** and a balanced mix of **summary and analytical visuals**, this dashboard supports strategic oversight, risk management, and informed decision-making within a global investment banking organization.

## Overview

The **Advisor & Desk Productivity Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of **advisor and desk-level performance** across Morgan Stanley. Using a **synthetic financial dataset**, the dashboard focuses on:

* How advisors and desks are performing in terms of revenue generation, AUM management, and net flows
* Productivity trends and cost-efficiency metrics at the advisor and desk levels
* Comparisons of performance across regions, client tiers, and risk profiles
* Identification of top-performing advisors, underperformers, and operational inefficiencies

The dashboard is designed for **senior leadership, operations managers, wealth management heads, and strategy teams** to monitor advisor performance, optimize resource allocation, and make data-driven decisions.

Through a combination of **executive KPIs, productivity-focused bar charts, trend analyses, scatter plots, and interactive gauges**, the dashboard delivers actionable insights into advisor and desk effectiveness.

---

## Business Storyline & Analytical Flow

### 1. Executive Snapshot – Advisor & Desk KPIs

The dashboard begins with **five KPI cards** providing an executive-level overview of productivity and efficiency:

* **Total Revenue (USD):** Revenue generated by all advisors within the selected context
* **Revenue per Advisor (USD):** Average revenue per advisor, highlighting individual performance
* **AUM per Advisor (USD):** Average assets under management per advisor
* **Cost-to-Revenue Ratio (%):** Efficiency metric showing cost of serving clients relative to revenue generated
* **Average Net Flows per Advisor (USD):** Cash generation effectiveness per advisor

These KPIs establish a **baseline understanding of scale, efficiency, and revenue productivity** for advisors and desks.

---

### 2. Advisor Revenue Distribution – Bar Chart

A **bar chart**, *Revenue by Advisor*, displays:

* Total revenue generated by individual advisors
* Sorted to highlight top-performing advisors
* Optional Top N filter to focus on key performers

This visual allows leadership to **quickly identify high-value advisors** and assess distribution of revenue across the advisory team.

---

### 3. Desk Performance Overview – Bar Chart

A **bar chart**, *Revenue by Desk*, provides a comparative view of revenue contributions across desks:

* Highlights which desks are generating the most revenue
* Can be segmented by client tier for deeper insights
* Helps allocate resources and prioritize high-performing teams

---

### 4. Advisor Productivity – Scatter Plot

A **scatter plot**, *Advisor Productivity (AUM vs Revenue)*, maps:

* **X-axis:** AUM per Advisor
* **Y-axis:** Revenue per Advisor
* **Bubble size:** Cost-to-Serve per Advisor
* **Color:** Client Risk Profile

This visual highlights **efficient advisors**, potential outliers, and advisors with high revenue but high servicing costs, supporting operational decisions.

---

### 5. Revenue Trend – Line Chart

A **line chart**, *Total Revenue Over Time by Desk*, visualizes:

* Revenue trends across reporting periods
* Comparison of desk performance over time
* Identifies desks with improving, declining, or stable revenue

This helps track **productivity trends and lifecycle performance** at the desk level.

---

### 6. Performance Benchmark – Gauge

A **gauge**, *Net Flows per Advisor vs Target*, provides:

* Quick visual check of advisor cash generation against target
* Color-coded ranges to highlight underperformers and overachievers
* Supports rapid decision-making and performance monitoring

---

### 7. Slicers – Interactive Filtering

All visuals are **fully interactive** and can be filtered using the following slicers:

* **Reporting Period:** Month, Quarter, Year
* **Region:** Geographic segmentation
* **Desk:** Desk-level analysis
* **Client Tier:** Segment-focused filtering
* **Risk Profile:** Risk-adjusted productivity insights

This dashboard enables stakeholders to:

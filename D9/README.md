## Overview

The **Compliance, AML and Operational Risk Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of **compliance incidents, AML risk, high-risk client concentration, and operational cost exposure** across Morgan Stanley’s client base. Built using a **synthetic financial dataset**, this dashboard focuses on:

- Monitoring **compliance incidents** and identifying trends over time  
- Assessing **AML risk** at client and segment levels  
- Highlighting **high-risk clients** and segments requiring focused oversight  
- Evaluating **operational cost exposure** as a proxy for operational risk  
- Supporting proactive decision-making for **risk management, compliance oversight, and operational efficiency**  

The dashboard is designed for **senior leadership, risk and compliance teams, and operational stakeholders** to support **risk monitoring, AML governance, and informed decision-making**.

By combining **executive KPIs, trend analysis, segmentation, risk-adjusted visuals, and interactive controls**, the dashboard delivers a clear and actionable view of compliance and operational risk.

---

## Business Storyline & Analytical Flow

### 1. Executive Snapshot – Risk KPIs

The dashboard opens with **five KPI cards** that provide an immediate understanding of enterprise risk:

- **Total Compliance Incidents:** Count of reported compliance issues  
- **% Accounts w/ Incidents:** Share of accounts affected by compliance events  
- **Avg AML Risk Score:** Average AML risk score across clients  
- **High-Risk Clients:** Count of clients with AML risk ≥ 75  
- **Avg Cost to Serve:** Average operational cost per account (USD), serving as a proxy for operational risk  

These KPIs establish a **baseline for overall compliance and operational risk** before deeper exploration.

---

### 2. Interactive Slicers – Contextual Control

All visuals respond dynamically to slicers:

- **Reporting Month**  
- **Region**  
- **Client Tier**  
- **Risk Profile**  
- **Desk**  

These controls allow stakeholders to explore compliance and risk patterns **across time, geography, segment, and risk categories**.

---

### 3. Compliance Incidents Trend – Line Chart

A **line chart**, *Compliance Incidents Trend Over Time*, visualizes:

- Month-over-month shifts in **compliance incident volume**  
- Emerging trends or seasonal patterns  
- Early detection of structural compliance risks  

This time-based view enables leadership to **track improvements or deterioration in compliance performance**.

---

### 4. Compliance Incidents by Region – Bar Chart

A **bar chart**, *Compliance Incidents by Region*, highlights:

- Which **geographies generate the most compliance issues**  
- Regional concentration risk  
- Guidance for **targeted compliance oversight and resource allocation**

---

### 5. AML Risk vs Revenue Exposure – Scatter Plot

A **scatter plot**, *AML Risk vs Revenue Exposure*, provides a risk-adjusted perspective:

- **X-axis:** Avg AML Risk Score  
- **Y-axis:** Total Revenue  
- **Bubble size:** Total AUM  
- **Detail:** Client ID  
- **Color (optional):** Client Tier  

This visual answers:
> *“Which high-risk clients are financially significant, and which are low impact?”*

---

### 6. Compliance Incidents by Client Tier – Bar Chart

A **bar chart**, *Compliance Incidents by Client Tier*, shows:

- Distribution of compliance issues across **client segments**  
- Helps leadership understand **tier-specific risk concentration**  
- Supports **advisor and operational focus**

---

### 7. Operational Cost Gauge – Gauge

A **gauge**, *Cost to Serve*, provides:

- Quick view of **average operational cost per account**  
- **Target vs actual** comparison to indicate operational strain  
- Conditional color ranges for **green (within target), yellow, and red (over target)**  

This complements KPIs and trend charts by highlighting **operational risk visually**.

---

## Key Takeaways

This dashboard enables stakeholders to:

- Monitor compliance incidents and AML risk trends over time  
- Identify high-risk clients and segments requiring immediate focus  
- Understand **geographic and tier-level concentration** of compliance issues  
- Assess operational cost exposure as a proxy for operational risk  
- Make **data-driven decisions** for compliance, AML oversight, and risk mitigation  

By integrating **KPIs, trend analysis, segmentation, scatter plots, and a risk gauge**, the dashboard delivers a **holistic, actionable view of compliance, AML, and operational risk**.

## Overview

The **Executive Financial Performance and Growth Dashboard** provides a **top-level view of enterprise financial performance** across Morgan Stanley. Built using a **synthetic financial dataset**, this dashboard focuses on:

- Monitoring **overall revenue and revenue composition**  
- Tracking **assets under management (AUM) and net flows** over time  
- Evaluating **revenue yield, cost efficiency, and risk metrics**  
- Supporting **data-driven strategic decisions at the executive level**  
- Providing **interactive navigation** to all other dashboards in the analytics suite  

The dashboard is designed for **senior leadership, strategy teams, and operations heads** to get a **clear, consolidated view of growth, efficiency, and risk**.

By combining **executive KPIs, trend analysis, composition charts, scatter plots, and navigation controls**, the dashboard delivers a **comprehensive, actionable view of enterprise performance**.

---

## Business Storyline & Analytical Flow

### 1. Executive Snapshot – KPIs

The dashboard opens with **six KPI cards** providing an immediate executive overview:

- **Total Revenue (USD):** Aggregate firm revenue  
- **Total AUM (USD):** Assets under management across clients  
- **Net Flows (USD):** Net capital inflows/outflows  
- **Revenue Yield (bps):** Revenue generated per unit of AUM  
- **Cost Ratio (%):** Cost efficiency of operations relative to revenue  
- **Average AML Risk Score:** Weighted risk exposure of revenue  

These KPIs establish a **baseline for enterprise performance** before deeper exploration.

---

### 2. Interactive Slicers – Contextual Control

All visuals respond dynamically to the following slicers:

- **Reporting Month**  
- **Year**  
- **Quarter**  
- **Region**  
- **Client Tier**  

These controls allow executives to explore performance **across time, geography, and client segments**.

---

### 3. Big Trend Visuals – Line / Combo Charts

**Total Revenue Trend** – Line Chart:  
- Visualizes month-over-month shifts in total revenue  
- Shows growth patterns and momentum  

**AUM & Net Flows Trend** – Combo Chart:  
- Tracks assets and net flows together  
- Highlights trends in client capital and asset growth

---

### 4. Revenue Composition

**Revenue by Business Line** – Stacked Column Chart:  
- Shows revenue split across **Fee Revenue, Trading Revenue, and IB Revenue**  
- Provides insight into what drives overall revenue  

**Revenue by Region** – Horizontal Bar Chart:  
- Breaks down total revenue by geographic region  
- Supports assessment of regional contribution and concentration

---

### 5. Risk & Efficiency Overlay

**Revenue vs AML Risk** – Scatter Plot:  
- X-axis: Average AML Risk Score  
- Y-axis: Total Revenue  
- Bubble size: Total AUM  
- Color: Region or Client Tier  
- Highlights **risk-adjusted revenue performance**

**Cost-to-Revenue vs Revenue** – Scatter/Bar Chart:  
- Shows cost efficiency relative to revenue by region  
- Supports **growth with discipline analysis**

---

### 6. Navigation Buttons – Vertical Strip

The dashboard includes **interactive buttons** for quick navigation to all other dashboards in the suite:

1. Wealth Management Performance  
2. Investment Banking Revenue and Pipeline  
3. Trading Performance and Market Risk  
4. Client Profitability and Lifecycle Analysis  
5. Advisor and Desk Productivity  
6. Portfolio Allocation Insights  
7. Client Experience and Engagement  
8. Compliance, AML and Operational Risk  
9. Client-Level Drill-Through Analytics  

These buttons turn the dashboard into a **control tower for the full analytics suite**.

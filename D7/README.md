## Overview

The **Portfolio Allocation Insights Dashboard** provides a strategic view of **how client assets are allocated across risk profiles, client tiers, and regions** at Morgan Stanley. Built using a **synthetic financial dataset**, this dashboard focuses on:

- Understanding the distribution of Assets Under Management (AUM) across varying risk appetites  
- Identifying concentration risk by region and client segment  
- Evaluating whether higher-risk portfolios are generating proportional revenue  
- Analyzing portfolio momentum through net flows over time  
- Assessing portfolio depth and quality through client-level asset concentration  

The dashboard is designed for **senior leadership, portfolio managers, risk teams, and strategy stakeholders** to support **capital allocation decisions, risk governance, and long-term portfolio strategy**.

By combining **executive KPIs, allocation visuals, momentum analysis, heatmaps, and trend-based insights**, the dashboard delivers a clear and actionable view of portfolio health and risk-return alignment.

---

## Business Storyline & Analytical Flow

### 1. Executive Snapshot – Portfolio KPIs

The dashboard opens with **five KPI cards** that establish an immediate, high-level understanding of the portfolio:

- **Total AUM (USD):** Overall assets under management within the selected context  
- **Net Flows (USD):** Net capital inflows or outflows, indicating portfolio momentum  
- **Total Revenue (USD):** Aggregate revenue generated from the portfolio  
- **Average AML Risk Score:** Portfolio-level risk proxy indicating overall risk exposure  
- **Average AUM per Client (USD):** Measure of portfolio depth and relationship quality  

Together, these KPIs provide a **baseline view of scale, growth, revenue quality, and risk posture** before deeper analysis.

---

### 2. AUM Allocation by Risk Profile – Stacked Column Chart

A **stacked column chart**, *AUM Allocation by Risk Profile and Client Tier*, visualizes:

- Distribution of AUM across **risk profiles**  
- Breakdown of each risk profile by **client tier**  
- Identification of concentration in aggressive or conservative portfolios  

This visual helps leadership understand **who holds risk** and whether portfolio exposure is balanced across client segments.

---

### 3. Portfolio Momentum – Scatter Plot (Net Flows vs AUM)

A **scatter plot**, *Portfolio Momentum: Net Flows vs AUM*, provides a dynamic assessment of portfolio health:

- **X-axis:** Total AUM  
- **Y-axis:** Net Flows  
- **Bubble size:** Total Revenue  
- **Color:** Client Tier  
- **Detail:** Risk Profile  

This visualization highlights:
- Strong portfolios (high AUM, positive flows)  
- At-risk portfolios (large AUM with sustained outflows)  
- Emerging growth opportunities with increasing inflows  

It enables leadership to distinguish **size-driven portfolios from momentum-driven growth**.

---

### 4. Portfolio Concentration – Region × Risk Heatmap

A **matrix heatmap**, *AUM Concentration by Region and Risk Profile*, shows:

- Geographic distribution of AUM across risk profiles  
- Areas of concentrated exposure  
- Regional dependence on aggressive or conservative investment strategies  

Conditional formatting transforms the matrix into a **risk concentration heatmap**, supporting portfolio diversification and regional risk governance.

---

### 5. Revenue Quality Check – Clustered Column Chart

A **clustered column chart**, *Revenue Contribution by Risk Profile and Region*, analyzes:

- Revenue generated by each risk profile  
- Regional contribution to portfolio revenue  
- Alignment (or misalignment) between portfolio risk and revenue returns  

This visual answers a critical strategic question:  
**Is higher portfolio risk actually paying off?**

---

### 6. Net Flows Trend Analysis – Line Chart

A **line chart**, *Net Flows Trend by Risk Profile*, tracks:

- Capital movement trends over reporting periods  
- Behavioral shifts in client risk appetite  
- Early warning signals of sustained outflows or risk aversion  

This time-based analysis helps leadership anticipate **structural changes in portfolio behavior** rather than reacting after the fact.

---

### 7. Portfolio Composition – Pie Chart

A **pie chart**, *AUM Distribution by Client Tier*, provides a quick composition snapshot:

- Share of total AUM by client tier  
- Immediate understanding of portfolio dependency on specific client segments  
- Executive-friendly overview for high-level discussions  

While not an analytical deep dive, this visual supports **rapid executive comprehension**.

---

### 8. Interactive Slicers – Contextual Control

All visuals are fully interactive and respond to the following slicers:

- **Region**  
- **Client Tier**  
- **Risk Profile**  

These slicers allow stakeholders to dynamically explore portfolio allocation patterns and risk exposure across different segments without losing analytical continuity.

---

## Key Takeaways

This dashboard enables stakeholders to:

- Understand how AUM is distributed across risk profiles and client segments  
- Identify portfolio concentration and regional risk exposure  
- Evaluate whether risk-taking is translating into revenue  
- Monitor capital flow momentum and early warning signals  
- Assess portfolio depth through average AUM per client  
- Support **risk-aware, data-driven portfolio strategy decisions**  

By integrating **allocation, momentum, concentration, revenue quality, and trend analysis**, the dashboard delivers a **holistic view of portfolio health** aligned with institutional decision-making standards.

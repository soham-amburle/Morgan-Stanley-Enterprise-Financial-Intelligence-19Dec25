Wealth Management Performance Dashboard

Morgan Stanley | Power BI | Synthetic Dataset

Overview

The Wealth Management Performance Dashboard is designed to provide senior leadership with a clear, structured view of Assets Under Management (AUM), client segmentation, advisor contribution, and revenue performance within a global wealth management organization.

Rather than focusing on isolated metrics, this dashboard tells a progressive business story — starting from overall scale, moving through client composition and growth trends, and concluding with advisor and segment-level value generation. The objective is to support strategic decision-making related to growth, client focus, and revenue optimization.

Business Storyline & Analytical Flow

1. Enterprise Snapshot – “How big are we and how are we performing?”
The dashboard opens with high-level KPI indicators that immediately establish the scale and health of the wealth management business:

Total Assets Under Management (AUM)

Net Client Flows

Wealth Management Revenue

Active Wealth Clients

These metrics give decision-makers an instant understanding of overall business size, growth momentum, and client base strength.

2. Geographic Context – “Where is this performance coming from?”
A Region slicer allows leaders to switch seamlessly between a global view and regional perspectives. This enables quick comparison across geographies while keeping the analysis consistent and standardized.

3. Client Segmentation – “Who holds the wealth?”
The AUM Distribution by Client Tier visual breaks down total assets across Retail, High Net Worth (HNW), and Ultra High Net Worth (UHNW) clients.
This helps leadership understand concentration risk, client mix, and dependency on specific wealth segments.

4. Growth Dynamics – “How is AUM evolving over time?”
The AUM Trend by Client Tier line chart adds a time dimension to the segmentation analysis.
By tracking AUM movement across client tiers over multiple periods, decision-makers can identify:

Sustained growth segments

Volatility or stagnation

Shifts in wealth concentration over time

5. Client Quality Indicator – “How valuable is the average client?”
The Average AUM per Client gauge translates aggregate numbers into per-client value.
This provides a quick assessment of portfolio depth and client quality, complementing the total AUM figures.

6. Advisor Performance – “Who is driving the assets?”
The Top 10 Advisors by AUM bar chart introduces accountability into the analysis.
It highlights top contributors, supports advisor benchmarking, and helps leadership identify high-performing relationship managers.

7. Client Behavior – “Where are clients adding or withdrawing money?”
Net Client Flows by Client Tier reveals inflows and outflows across segments.
This visual connects client behavior directly to segmentation, indicating confidence, retention, or risk within each tier.

8. Monetization – “Which segments generate revenue?”
Fee Revenue by Client Tier closes the analytical loop by linking assets and flows to actual revenue outcomes.
This allows leadership to assess whether asset concentration and client growth are translating into sustainable revenue.

Key Takeaway

This dashboard transforms raw wealth management data into a clear executive narrative — from overall scale and growth, to client composition, advisor contribution, and revenue realization. It enables leaders to quickly understand where the wealth is, how it is moving, and who is creating value.

Tools & Data

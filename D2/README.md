# Wealth Management Performance Dashboard

**Organization:** Morgan Stanley  
**Tool:** Power BI  
**Dataset:** Synthetic Wealth Management Data (100,000 records)  
**Dashboard Focus:** Assets Under Management (AUM) & Client Segmentation  
**Date:** 27–30 December 2025  
**Created by:** Soham S. Amburle  

---

## Overview

The **Wealth Management Performance Dashboard** is designed to provide senior leadership with a clear, structured view of how assets, clients, advisors, and revenue interact within a global wealth management business.

Rather than presenting isolated metrics, this dashboard follows a **business-first narrative**, starting with enterprise-level scale, moving through client segmentation and growth trends, and concluding with advisor contribution and revenue realization. The goal is to enable data-driven strategic decisions around growth, client focus, and monetization.

---

## Business Storyline & Analytical Flow

### 1. Enterprise Snapshot – Business Scale & Momentum
The dashboard opens with high-level KPI indicators that establish the overall size and health of the wealth management business:
- Total Assets Under Management (AUM)
- Net Client Flows
- Wealth Management Revenue
- Active Wealth Clients  

These KPIs answer the first executive question:  
**“How big are we, and are we growing?”**

---

### 2. Geographic Context – Regional Perspective
A **Region slicer** allows decision-makers to view performance at a global level or focus on specific regions. This ensures consistent analysis while enabling regional comparisons and localized insights.

---

### 3. Client Segmentation – Where the Wealth Resides
The **AUM Distribution by Client Tier** visual breaks down total assets across Retail, High Net Worth (HNW), and Ultra High Net Worth (UHNW) clients.  
This helps leadership understand asset concentration, dependency on specific segments, and overall client mix.

---

### 4. Growth Dynamics – AUM Over Time
The **AUM Trend by Client Tier** line chart introduces a time dimension to segmentation.  
It shows how assets within each client tier evolve over time, highlighting sustained growth, volatility, or shifts in wealth concentration.

---

### 5. Client Quality – Portfolio Depth
The **Average AUM per Client** gauge translates aggregate AUM into a per-client metric.  
This provides a quick indicator of portfolio depth and client quality, complementing the total AUM figures.

---

### 6. Advisor Performance – Accountability & Contribution
The **Top 10 Advisors by AUM** bar chart identifies the highest-contributing advisors.  
This visual supports advisor benchmarking, performance monitoring, and accountability within the wealth management organization.

---

### 7. Client Behavior – Capital Movement
The **Net Client Flows by Client Tier** chart highlights inflows and outflows across client segments.  
It helps assess client confidence, retention, and behavioral trends across different wealth tiers.

---

### 8. Monetization – Revenue Realization
The **Fee Revenue by Client Tier** chart closes the analytical loop by linking assets and flows to revenue outcomes.  
It enables leadership to evaluate whether asset concentration and growth are translating into sustainable revenue.

---

## Key Takeaway

This dashboard delivers a clear executive narrative — from overall scale and growth, to client composition, advisor contribution, and revenue generation. It enables leaders to quickly understand **where the wealth is, how it is moving, and who is creating value** within the wealth management business.

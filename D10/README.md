**Organization:** Morgan Stanley

**Dataset:** Synthetic Financial Data – Client-Level Focus  
**Dashboard Focus:** Client Revenue, Profitability, Net Flows, AUM, NPS, and Advisor Performance

---

## Overview

The **Client-Level Drill-Through Analytics Dashboard** provides a **360° view of individual client performance, engagement, and profitability** across Morgan Stanley’s client base. Built using a **synthetic financial dataset**, this dashboard focuses on:

- Monitoring **client revenue and revenue composition**  
- Tracking **assets under management (AUM) and net flows** over time  
- Evaluating **profitability and advisor contribution**  
- Understanding **client satisfaction and engagement via NPS scores**  
- Supporting **data-driven decisions for client strategy, retention, and resource allocation**

The dashboard is designed for **senior leadership, relationship managers, wealth management heads, and strategy teams** to support **client-level insights and targeted decision-making**.

By combining **executive KPIs, trend analysis, revenue breakdowns, and interactive controls**, the dashboard delivers a **clear, actionable view of client performance**.

---

## Business Storyline & Analytical Flow

### 1. Executive Snapshot – Client KPIs

The dashboard opens with **six KPI cards** that provide an immediate understanding of the selected client:

- **Total Revenue (USD):** Aggregate revenue for the client  
- **Total AUM (USD):** Assets under management for the client  
- **Net Flows (USD):** Net capital inflows/outflows  
- **Fee Revenue (USD):** Revenue from fees  
- **Trading Revenue (USD):** Revenue from trading activities  
- **Average NPS Score:** Measure of client satisfaction  

These KPIs establish a **baseline for overall client performance** before deeper exploration.

---

### 2. Interactive Slicers – Contextual Control

All visuals respond dynamically to the following slicers:

- **Reporting Month**  
- **Year**  
- **Quarter**  
- **Region**  
- **Client Tier**  
- **Risk Profile**  
- **Advisor**  

These controls allow stakeholders to explore client performance **across time, geography, segment, and advisor**.

---

### 3. Revenue Trend – Line Chart

A **line chart**, *Client Revenue Trend Over Time*, visualizes:

- Month-over-month shifts in **total revenue**  
- Emerging growth patterns or fluctuations  
- Enables leadership to **track revenue evolution for the selected client**

---

### 4. Revenue Breakdown – Pie Chart

A **pie chart**, *Revenue by Source*, displays:

- Revenue split across **Fee Revenue, Trading Revenue, and IB Revenue**  
- Shows proportion of each revenue stream contributing to total client revenue  
- Supports **quick assessment of revenue composition**

---

### 5. Net Flows vs AUM – Clustered Column Chart

A **clustered column chart** visualizes:

- **Total AUM** vs **Net Flows** over time  
- Highlights asset growth, retention, and capital movement trends  
- Supports **client-level insights on asset dynamics**

---

### 6. Client Satisfaction – Gauge

A **gauge**, *Client Satisfaction (NPS)*, provides:

- Quick at-a-glance view of **average client NPS score**  
- Conditional color ranges indicate **healthy, caution, or at-risk client sentiment**  

---

## Key Takeaways

This dashboard enables stakeholders to:

- Monitor **client-level revenue, AUM, and net flows** over time  
- Assess **revenue composition and profitability** by client  
- Understand **advisor contribution and client engagement**  
- Track **client satisfaction using NPS**  
- Make **data-driven decisions for client strategy, retention, and operational focus**

By integrating **KPIs, trend analysis, pie charts, column charts, gauges, and slicers**, the dashboard delivers a **holistic, actionable view of individual client performance**.

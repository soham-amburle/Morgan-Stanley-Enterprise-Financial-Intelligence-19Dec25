## Overview

The **Trading Performance and Market Risk Dashboard** provides a consolidated view of trading revenue, growth trends, desk and regional contributions, client segment performance, and associated risk exposure within Morgan Stanley’s trading business.

Designed in **Power BI** using a synthetic financial dataset, this dashboard focuses on understanding:

- How trading revenue is generated and evolving over time  
- Which desks, regions, or client segments contribute most to trading revenue  
- Risk-adjusted performance using weighted AML risk scores  
- Efficiency metrics such as revenue per account and progress against revenue targets  

The dashboard is intended for trading leadership, regional heads, risk and compliance teams, and strategy stakeholders to monitor trading revenue, risk-adjusted performance, and operational efficiency. Through a combination of **executive KPIs, trend analysis, segmentation-based visuals, and risk overlays**, it supports informed decision-making and sustainable revenue growth.

---

## Business Storyline & Analytical Flow

### 1. Executive Snapshot – Trading Performance KPIs
The dashboard begins with **four KPI cards** that provide an instant executive view of trading performance:

- **Total Trading Revenue (USD):** Overall revenue generated from trading activities  
- **Trading Revenue Growth (QoQ %):** Quarter-over-quarter growth in trading revenue  
- **Trading Risk-Weighted Score:** AML risk score weighted by trading revenue, showing risk-adjusted exposure  
- **Trading Revenue per Account:** Average revenue contribution per trading account  

These KPIs establish both the **scale of trading revenue** and the **risk-adjusted efficiency** of the trading book. Leadership can quickly gauge overall revenue, growth momentum, risk exposure, and revenue efficiency per account.

---

### 2. Temporal Dynamics – Revenue Trends
A **line chart** visualizes **Trading Revenue Trend Over Time by Region**, highlighting:

- Growth patterns and seasonal fluctuations  
- Performance differences across regions  
- Trend insights for desks and advisors when slicers are applied  

---

### 3. Cross-Sectional Analysis – Desk and Regional Performance
Two **clustered column charts** provide detailed cross-sectional insights:

- **Trading Revenue by Desk and Region:** Shows contribution of desks and regions to overall trading revenue, identifying top-performing teams  
- **Trading Revenue vs Risk by Desk and Region:** Combines revenue magnitude with weighted risk exposure, allowing for **risk-adjusted performance assessment**

---

### 4. Client-Level Insights – Revenue Segmentation
A **treemap** visual, **Trading Revenue by Client Segment**, shows:

- Contribution of client tiers and risk profiles to trading revenue  
- Identification of high-revenue / high-risk client segments  
- Insights for client segmentation and strategic engagement  

---

### 5. Target Performance – Gauge
A **gauge visual** tracks **Trading Revenue vs Target**, giving leadership an **at-a-glance measure** of whether trading revenue goals are being met. It supports dynamic filtering via slicers.

---

### 6. Slicers – Interactive Filtering
All visuals are dynamic and can be filtered using interactive slicers:

- **Time Filter (Month / Quarter):** Filter by reporting period  
- **Region:** Filter by geography  
- **Advisor:** Filter by individual advisors  
- **Desk:** Filter by trading desk

## Key Takeaways

This dashboard delivers a **structured, executive-ready view** of trading performance, efficiency, and risk. It enables stakeholders to:

- Understand where trading revenue is generated  
- Assess growth and efficiency of desks and advisors  
- Identify high-risk areas that may require supervision  
- Make informed, risk-adjusted strategic decisions  

By combining **high-level KPIs, revenue trends, desk and region analysis, client segmentation, and risk-adjusted analytics**, the dashboard provides a comprehensive view for leadership decision-making.  

With **interactive slicers** and a balanced mix of **summary and analytical visuals**, this dashboard supports strategic oversight, risk management, and sustainable growth within Morgan Stanley’s trading business.

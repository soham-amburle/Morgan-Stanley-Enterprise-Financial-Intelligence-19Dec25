## Overview

The **Client Experience & Engagement Dashboard** provides a strategic view of **how clients perceive Morgan Stanley’s service quality across tiers, regions, and risk profiles**. Built using a **synthetic financial dataset**, this dashboard focuses on:

- Monitoring client satisfaction via **NPS scores** and complaints  
- Identifying high-risk clients and segments requiring intervention  
- Evaluating trends in client sentiment over time  
- Understanding client experience differences by **tier, region, and risk profile**  
- Supporting proactive decision-making for **advisor management, service quality, and retention strategy**  

The dashboard is designed for **senior leadership, client experience teams, relationship managers, and operational stakeholders** to support **service improvement initiatives, risk mitigation, and client retention efforts**.

By combining **executive KPIs, trend analysis, segmentation, composition visuals, and interactive controls**, the dashboard delivers a clear and actionable view of client engagement health.

---

## Business Storyline & Analytical Flow

### 1. Executive Snapshot – Client KPIs

The dashboard opens with **six KPI cards** that provide an immediate understanding of client experience:

- **Average NPS Score:** Overall client sentiment across selected filters  
- **Total Complaints:** Volume of complaints received during the reporting period  
- **% Clients with Complaints:** Spread of dissatisfaction among clients  
- **High-Risk Client Experience Rate:** Clients with low NPS or complaints, indicating potential churn risk  
- **Avg Complaints per Client:** Average complaints per client to measure intensity of friction  
- **% Detractor Clients:** Share of clients scoring below 0 on NPS, highlighting sentiment risk  

These KPIs establish a **baseline for overall client experience before deeper exploration**.

---

### 2. NPS Trend Analysis – Line Chart

A **line chart**, *NPS Trend Over Time*, visualizes:

- Monthly shifts in **average client satisfaction**  
- Emerging patterns in engagement, signaling improvement or deterioration  
- Early detection of structural client sentiment issues  

This time-based view allows leadership to **track progress and effectiveness of interventions**.

---

### 3. Complaints by Client Tier – Bar Chart

A **bar chart**, *Complaints by Client Tier*, highlights:

- Which client tiers are generating the most complaints  
- Potential operational or service issues within specific segments  
- Segmentation insight to guide **tier-specific service strategies**  

This visual ensures stakeholders understand **where service quality risks are concentrated**.

---

### 4. Client Experience Risk Matrix – Scatter Plot

A **scatter plot**, *NPS vs Complaints (Client-Level View)*, provides a nuanced perspective:

- **X-axis:** Total complaints per client  
- **Y-axis:** Average NPS score per client  
- **Bubble size:** Total AUM (optional)  
- **Detail:** Client ID  

This plot identifies four key quadrants:

1. High NPS / Low Complaints → Healthy clients  
2. High NPS / High Complaints → Loyal but frustrated  
3. Low NPS / Low Complaints → Silent risk  
4. Low NPS / High Complaints → Critical risk  

It allows leadership to **prioritize intervention and advisor focus**.

---

### 5. Client Distribution by NPS Category – Pie Chart

A **pie chart**, *Client Distribution by NPS Category*, shows:

- Proportion of **Promoters, Passives, and Detractors**  
- Immediate executive-friendly view of client sentiment composition  
- Distribution across client tiers, regions, and risk profiles via slicers  

This visual complements KPIs with a **clear visual composition of engagement health**.

---

### 6. Interactive Slicers – Contextual Control

All visuals respond dynamically to four slicers:

- **Reporting Month**  
- **Client Tier**  
- **Region**  
- **Risk Profile**  

These controls allow stakeholders to explore client experience patterns **across time, segment, and risk dimensions**.
